About Me

Hi, I’m Pol. I live in Bucharest and have worked as a SOC analyst at RevelSI since August 2026. I completed my bachelor’s degree in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Bucharest in 2026.

I use Microsoft Azure, Microsoft Defender, and Splunk in my day-to-day work. What draws me to the SOC is piecing together what happened from the evidence available. That work also makes me curious about the engineering behind it, especially how teams build better detections and automate repetitive steps in an investigation.

Outside work, I enjoy board games and reading about theology and psychology. My Christian faith is an important part of my life.
